A Hit

Solid strike lands, forcing grunt of pain.
Opponent’s defense slowed but not broken.
Glancing but bloody cut.
Forceful impact jars the foe’s stance.
Blow lands but is partly absorbed by armor.
Attacker pushes foe back a pace.
Defensive weapon battered aside, leaving small opening.
Quick follow-through cuts flesh, but not deep.
Opponent reels but keeps footing.
Weapon sinks into opponent’s guard, bruising through block.
