A Miss

Attack narrowly slips past — defender feels air move.
Clean parry, ringing clash of steel or wood.
Sidestep carries defender just out of reach.
Attack intercepted mid-swing, momentum lost.
Weapon caught on opponent’s guard and shoved aside.
Attacker’s strike slides harmlessly across armor.
Defender angles body, attack whiffs wide.
Defender redirects the blow into empty space.
Attacker’s strike hesitates, no opening found.
Clash ends in bind — both weapons locked together.
