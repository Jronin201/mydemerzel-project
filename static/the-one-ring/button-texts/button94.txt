Defender Fail

Parry too late — blow lands partially.
Guard broken, impact bruises through.
Sidestep mistimed, grazed hit lands.
Defense absorbs some force but weapon slips through.
Arm jarred painfully by half-block.
Dodged but still clipped by weapon tip.
Retreat too slow — attacker pushes in.
Crossed weapons slide, strike still connects.
Low defense misses high attack.
Footwork falters, position worsens after block.
