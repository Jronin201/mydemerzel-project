A Crit Hit

Strike lands with devastating force, staggering the foe.
Blow bypasses defense entirely, hitting a vital spot.
Expert precision: weapon finds a gap in armor.
Opponent’s guard shattered — weapon arm numbed or weakened.
Attacker drives opponent back several steps.
Weapon impact disarms or nearly wrenches foe’s weapon away.
Perfect timing: riposte lands before opponent can recover.
Stunning strike: opponent dazed, vision reeling.
Deep wound inflicted — attacker’s weapon lodges with brutal effect.
Crushing blow drops opponent to knees.
