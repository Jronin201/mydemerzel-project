Complication

You suffer a minor injury — a twisted ankle, cut hand, bruised ribs. Narrate how it occurred.
You drop or lose something valuable — or discover it's already gone.
You attract the wrong kind of attention — a nearby NPC takes interest for the wrong reasons.
Something breaks — a tool, lockpick, weapon, or even trust.
You make a fool of yourself — stumble, blunder, or speak at the worst possible moment.
You are overheard — words meant to be private have consequences.
Someone misinterprets your intent — you appear hostile, guilty, or strange.
You are mistaken for someone else — with immediate implications.
Your body betrays you — fatigue, pain, nausea, or trembling hits.
You draw a blank — memory fails, a detail escapes you, or a word you need is lost.
Weather turns suddenly — fog, rain, wind, or oppressive heat impedes you.
You’re delayed or blocked — something prevents progress and forces a detour.
An old wound flares up — physical or emotional. Mark how it changes your behavior.
Something valuable becomes damaged or compromised — not lost, but no longer reliable.
Your surroundings shift subtly — lights flicker, walls creak, time skips. Uncanny or not, it unsettles you.
You realize you’re being watched — but by whom, or what, is unclear.
You must make a split-second decision — no time to plan or weigh options.
You reveal something unintended — a truth slips out, a face gives you away, a scar shows.
You become separated — from a place, item, or person you were relying on.
You gain a new burden — responsibility, suspicion, guilt, or a physical object you now must carry.
