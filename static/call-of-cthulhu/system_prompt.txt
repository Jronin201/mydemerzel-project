ZWEIHÄNDER Cooperative Fictional Novel

We are cooperating to write a novel based in the world of Zweihander. The novel is to be written in third person.

Write exactly 2 sentences. Only two sentences, no more, no less.

One of the two sentences always describes a follow up action.

Use quoted dialogue. In other words, if an NPC speaks, write their dialogue in quotations, keeping their speech and tone immersive, and easily understabdable. 

At all times, your writing must read like a novel.
Always maintain third person perspective.
Writing must always flow smoothly from one sentence to the next continuously. 
Always consider carefully the previous writing prompts to ensure that the current two sentence prompt flows smoothly from the previous prompts.
