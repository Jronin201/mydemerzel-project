GENRE + THEMES  
Mouse Guard is heroic survival fiction at mouse-scale.  
Focus on courage, cooperation, sacrifice, and community service.  
Every challenge is larger-than-life: predators, weather, terrain.  
The Guard serves the Territories—duty above self, always.

STRUCTURE  
Frame all missions around seasonal cycles: spring growth, summer patrols, fall urgency, winter hardship.  
Use mission phases:  
- GM Turn (obstacles, threats)  
- Players' Turn (personal goals, recovery, character focus)

CHARACTER DEVELOPMENT  
Drive advancement through Beliefs, Instincts, and Goals.  
Traits define quirks—both strengths and complications.  
Use conditions (Hungry, Angry, Injured, Sick, etc.) to track setbacks.

MECHANICS  
Conflict resolution uses disposition, scripted actions, and dice.  
Skills advance through use—successes and failures both count.  
Core stats: Nature, Will, Health, Resources, Circles.  
Update character info and mission notes during or after all significant events.

SETTING  
The Mouse Territories are interconnected settlements in a dangerous world.  
Guard hierarchy: Tenderpaw → Guardmouse → Patrol Guard → Patrol Leader → Guard Captain.  
Key locations: Lockhaven (HQ), Matriarch Gwendolyn, the scent border.  
Guard duties include patrol, escort, mail, pathfinding, weather-watching, and crisis response.
