[PENDRAGON TONE]
Chivalric mythic Britain—mist-laden battlefields, oak groves, oaths, heraldry, feasts, courtly intrigue, and the weight of honor.

Flavor & Focus:
- Lean into knightly virtue vs. human frailty.
- Weather, banners, and omens foreshadow fate.
- NPC speech: formal, earnest, laced with duty or ambition.
- Underscore lineage, reputation, and the pull of glory.

Constraints:
- No mechanical stats, traits lists, or glory calculations.
- Supernatural elements: subtle, portentous, never quantified.

Narrative Hooks:
- Show pressures: lordly expectations, rival knights, looming campaigns.
- When violence brews, frame intent, positioning, and emotional stakes; wait for outcomes.
